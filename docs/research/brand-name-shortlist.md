# Brand name shortlist

Current working name:
- CMS Platform

Preferred candidate:
- OutletOS
  - Broad, product-like, and not limited to cafes or canteens.
  - Can support future expansion into restaurants, canteens, counters, kiosks, and small retail operations.

Other candidates:
- Counterbook
  - Strong fit for counter operations plus ledger workflows.

- Servewise
  - Clean, trustworthy, and less technical.

- VendorDesk
  - Generic B2B direction; can expand beyond food vendors.

- CanteenDesk
  - Direct and readable for the first target market.

- OrderLedger
  - Clear operations plus finance signal.

- ServeStack
  - Modern SaaS feel while staying restaurant-related.

- KitchenBook
  - Friendly and easy to understand in the Indian food-service context.

- CounterDesk
  - Simple, operational, and practical.

- FoodOps
  - Very clear, but more generic.

Decision notes:
- Run trademark and domain checks before committing to a public launch name.
- Avoid names that lock the product only to cafes if the long-term goal is broader vendor operations.
- Prefer names that sound useful to small business owners, not only software teams.
